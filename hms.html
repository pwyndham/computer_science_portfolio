Project Overview

HMS was my classmates and my Hosptial Management System from my Database Systems Class at UTSA. I took the role of Project Manager as well as lead Database designer. I designed the database schmea while the others gave feedback to myself and Bryan who was working on the frontend portion of the project. I used MySQL Workbench to create the ERD diagram and implement the database using MySQL. I used Lucid chart to plan out the ERD, and Trello to manage our tasks. At the end for our final deliverable I used a video editing software called Shotcut to compile all our work into a final presentation video.

The application only used a MySQL database with a direct connection to the front-end which Bryan used Bootstrap to create views.
💡 Challenges & Solutions
Challenge: Fine tuning the ERD and working through the relationships.

Solution: We held several brainstorming sessions as a group to discuss the relationships and how to best implement them.

Learning Outcomes

  • Gained experience working with a vast environment that is Hospital Operations
  • Streamlining daily operations across multiple departments; Including areas such as: Patient records, doctor scheduling, billing and inventory management
  • Improved skills in database design and optimization
  • Managing patient data, including records, appointments and medical history
  • Intended to enhance patient care through effective data management
  • Maintain priority when maintaining sensitive healthcare data
